# Tendrils CRM 4.9.10
- Who created a ticket: new "Created by" column in the Tickets table (employees), "Created by" row in the ticket window, and a "Created by" card at the top of the Route tab.
- Route tab: summary strip (created by, team, assignee, tasks done), shows the latest 8 steps with "Show earlier steps" / "Show fewer", creator + date on the ticket card.
- Ticket and task windows are larger (up to 1500x1000, wider side panel, tighter spacing). In the ticket window "Flag & remind" and "Add linked task" now sit at the top of the right panel, and the linked-task list scrolls inside its own box, so nothing needs scrolling to reach them.
- Frontend-only. No API or database changes.
