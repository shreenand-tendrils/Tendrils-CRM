Privacy Policy

Tendrils Operations · workspace.tendrilsioservices.com

Tendrils Operations is a private workspace used by Tendrils and its invited clients to manage tickets, tasks, projects and files. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google we receive only your name, email address and profile picture. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts or any other Google data.

We also store the content you add to the workspace: tickets, tasks, comments, chat messages, notes and uploaded files, together with an activity log of who changed what.

How we use it

Only to let you sign in, show you the work you are allowed to see, send workspace emails (invitations, assignments, reminders) and keep an audit trail. Access is invitation-only and role-based; clients only see their own company's work.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising. Data is shared only with the people in your workspace who have access, and with service providers needed to run the system (hosting, database and email delivery).

Storage and security

Data is stored on Tendrils-managed servers. Sign-in uses Google OAuth; we never see your Google password.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account or project is active. To have your account or data removed, email us at the address below.
